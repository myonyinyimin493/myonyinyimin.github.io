01 · Stop Online HarmLive in production

Online Ambulance: reporting platform and case dashboard

ProblemWhen I took over, the public reporting site was unusable and caseworkers in four countries had no safe way to share cases.
What I builtRebuilt the reporting site in about 6 weeks, then a case dashboard where each team sees only its own country's cases.
  • Secure report form with a case number for every survivor
  • Role-based access by country and role, plus a cross-border role for coordinators
  • Team notes with @mentions, an evidence tab, and an English/Burmese feedback form
  • Email moved from AWS SES to Postmark, with delivery and bounce tracking by webhook
